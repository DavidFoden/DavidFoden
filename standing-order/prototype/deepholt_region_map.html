Deepholt

Fifteen chambers, twenty-three tunnels, no corridor. Every camp can be reached by at least two routes, so no two groups meet the cavern in the same order. Tap any chamber.

unclaimed Sporewardens Ashfang Mirelurk Assayers Circle size = how much food the chamber can carry Dotted line = the trader's circuit

Tap a chamber to read it. The four camps sit far apart on purpose — whichever one you meet first becomes your playthrough's spine.

Why the shape matters. A corridor forces the order you meet camps and gives each camp only one or two neighbours, which starves the politics. This layout gives most chambers three connections and several long loops, so camps share contested borders, hunting ranges overlap in more places, and a route you open in the north can change who eats in the south.
